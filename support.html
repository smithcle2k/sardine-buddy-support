Support & account help

Email runtopetes@gmail.com. Sardine Buddy is operated by Cleaven Smith.

Report a price or store problem

In the app, sign in and use the report action on a deal. You can also email the product, retailer, store or ZIP, offer date and what appears incorrect. Please avoid personal information in reports. We cannot guarantee store inventory or honor a retailer’s price.

Password recovery

In Profile, enter your account email, tap “Forgot password?” and request a recovery code. Enter the email code and a new password. If an email does not arrive, check spam and request another code later. Never send support your password or recovery code.

Delete your account

In Profile, choose “Delete account,” enter your current password, then confirm permanent deletion. This removes the account, watchlist, price targets, device notification registrations and reports from the live service. Browsing remains available without an account. If you cannot access the app, email us from the account address to request deletion; we may need to verify ownership.

Location and notifications

Location is optional. Use ZIP/state filters instead, or change location access in iPhone Settings. Turn off “Notify on this iPhone” in Profile to remove that registration. You can also disable notifications in iPhone Settings. Removing an alert target stops future matching for that target.

When contacting support

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description of the issue. Remove personal information from screenshots. Never include passwords, sign-in links, verification codes, payment details or private documents.
