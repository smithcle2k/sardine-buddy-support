Privacy policy

Sardine Buddy is operated by Cleaven Smith. Contact runtopetes@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. This policy describes the iPhone app and this support website. The app is in development; these pages do not announce a public release.

Browse without an account

You can browse deals without signing in. We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs. This website has no analytics scripts, cookies, contact form or embedded third-party content. GitHub Pages hosts it and processes technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Information used by the app

  • Accounts: your email address, account identifier and authentication information provide sign-in, recovery and private features. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; the app does not save your password. Session credentials are stored in the iPhone’s Keychain.
  • Saved products and targets: we store your watchlist, exact package-price targets and chosen target ZIP, state and optional retailer so we can show saved items and match offers. These are private to your account.
  • Notifications: if you opt in, we store an Apple push token, installation identifier, device preference and delivery records to send price alerts. Apple processes push delivery. Notifications use generic text without your email, target location or price.
  • Reports: we store the deal, reason and text you submit, plus review information. Authorized operators can review reports to correct listings. Please do not include sensitive information.
  • Technical requests: the backend and public image hosts receive your IP address and requested resources when serving the app. Infrastructure providers may retain security and operational logs.

Optional location

Location access is requested when you choose to use it. Coordinates stay in memory on your iPhone and are cleared when the app enters the background. They are not sent to our backend. Manual browse ZIP/state filters are not saved. ZIP/state information you save with a price target is stored for matching.

Why and with whom information is used

We use information to operate accounts, provide private saves and alerts, review deal problems, respond to support and protect the service. Supabase provides the app’s database, authentication and storage. Apple provides optional notifications. GitHub hosts this website. Public product images and source links may use other hosts; opening a retailer or source link is subject to that site’s practices. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect the service.

Retention and deletion

Account information and saves remain while your account exists or until you remove them. Remove saves and targets in Watchlist. Turning off device notifications in Profile removes that registration and its associated delivery records. Reports remain for review while the account exists; deleting the account removes those reports and their review audit copies. Account deletion also removes authentication records, watchlists, targets, notification registrations and delivery records from the live service. Non-personal catalog, deal and price history may remain.

Restricted backup copies and infrastructure security logs can persist until provider retention periods expire. They are not used to restore deleted account features during normal operation. We must reapply account deletions if a backup is restored. Contact us for the applicable provider retention settings or a deletion request. Support emails remain as needed to answer and document your request; you can ask us to remove correspondence, subject to legal obligations.

Your choices and requests

Use Profile → Delete account to permanently delete your account after confirming your password. Browsing still works without signing in. If you cannot access your account, email us from its address; we may verify ownership before acting. You can request access, correction or deletion by email. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. You can revoke location or notification permission in iPhone Settings.

Security and children

We use encrypted connections, account access rules and restricted operator access. No system guarantees absolute security. Sardine Buddy is intended for general shopping use and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel.
