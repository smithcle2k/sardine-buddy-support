Help with Sardine Buddy

Sardine Buddy is an iPhone deal discovery app for comparing verified canned sardine offers, package prices and prices per ounce. It is currently in development.

Sardine Buddy does not sell products or process orders. Store participation, offer conditions and dates matter. A listed offer does not promise stock.
